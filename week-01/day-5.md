## 📖 Lesson Content

### 1. Table Tags

```html id="p1t4e6"
<table bord="1">

<caption>Student Data</caption>

<thead>
<tr>
<th>Name</th>
<th>Class</th>
</tr>
</thead>

<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Ali</td>
<td>8</td>
</tr>
</tbody>

<tfoot>
<tr>
<td colspan="2">End</td>
</tr>
</tfoot>

</table>
```
